RECENZIJA

Grips you from the first minute and refuses to let go. Whether you're struck with fear by the ever-relevant depictions of militarized violence, or you're laughing at the hundredth dadfailure Leo moment,
you're in for an absolutely unforgettable time at the movies. I seriously feel bad for every other film that comes out this year.

PLOT

Bob is a washed-up revolutionary who lives in a state of stoned paranoia, surviving off-grid with his spirited and self-reliant daughter, Willa. When his evil nemesis resurfaces and Willa goes missing, the former radical scrambles to find her as both father and daughter battle the consequences of their pasts.
