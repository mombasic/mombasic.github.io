RECENZIJA

now this is exactly the kind of wacky dark comedy i needed in my life right now. but this is really unlike any other scorsese movie i’ve seen, so off the wall.
at first i was thinking it was a little weird, but man the intertwining storyline swept me away. i felt myself slowly losing my mind with paul through the night.
hopelessly funny, and every line delivered is gold.
and the growing cast of characters all eventually wanting to kill him is just hilarious

PLOT

Paul meets Marcy at a coffee shop after work, and gets her phone number. He calls her, she asks him to come over, and things take a turn for the bizarre. Paul spends the rest of the night trying to get home, dealing with angry cabbies, dead women (and their bartender husbands), clumsy catburglars, quirky sculptresses, unstable waitresses, condescending bouncers, and irate mobs led by ice cream truck drivers along the way.
