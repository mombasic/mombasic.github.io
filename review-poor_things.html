RECENZIJA

absolutely movie of the year for me. unbelievably funny and thoughtful and gorgeous, i wanted to pause on every frame!
every character is rendered with such care and depth and without a hint of morality placed on their actions for the audience.

PLOT

From filmmaker Yorgos Lanthimos and producer Emma Stone comes the incredible tale and fantastical evolution of Bella Baxter (Stone), a young woman crafted by the brilliant and unorthodox scientist Dr. Godwin Baxter (Willem Dafoe). Under Baxter's protection, Bella is eager to learn. Hungry for the worldliness she is lacking, Bella runs off with Duncan Wedderburn (Mark Ruffalo), a slick and debauched lawyer, on a whirlwind adventure across the continents. Free from the prejudices of her times, Bella experiences trials and a desire for equality.
